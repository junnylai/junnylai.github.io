Gemrein is the independent design label of the Huayi group, relaunched in 2025 from huayi·mdn. The brief was a brand "with blue blood": elegant rebellion against the ordinary. The site opens in a palazzo with a single cobalt sofa and lets each room speak in full-screen scenes.

Elegant rebellion. The enemy of the ordinary.
