Huayi Space

Brand website and campaign
Role
Brand design, visual direction, content selection and development. Led the team that produced the content.

huayispace.vip

Huayi Space is a Shenzhen furniture house built on original design. I led its digital flagship from brand design to code, then built an AI campaign that takes one bouclé sofa out of the showroom and onto a sea cliff, into the desert and through raw concrete.

Doing one thing to the extreme beats doing ten thousand things averagely.

Brand line

The sofa, anywhere

A single product held perfectly consistent across five worlds: the bouclé texture, the walnut trim and the cushion layout survive every scene. No set builds, no location shoots.
